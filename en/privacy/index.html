Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. General provisions

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how personal data of visitors to https://svetlana.is-a.dev/en/ (the “Website”) is processed.

The data controller is an individual, Svetlana Olegovna Kochetova (the “Controller”). You can contact the Controller at svekochetova@gmail.com.

The Policy is prepared in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data” (Russian Federation). If the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, you may also have the rights it provides; to exercise them, please contact the Controller by email.

By using the Website and providing your data to the Controller, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website.

2. What data is processed

The Website has no contact forms, registration or payment, so no data is collected through forms. The Controller receives data only in the following cases:

  • when you book a call via Calendly: your name, email address, the selected date and time, and any information you add yourself;
  • when you contact the Controller via messengers (Telegram, WhatsApp, MAX) or by email: your name or username, phone number or email address, and the content of the conversation;
  • when you visit the Website: technical data automatically received by the analytics service (IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, time of the visit). See the Cookie Policy for details.

3. Purposes of processing

  • to communicate with you and respond to your requests;
  • to arrange and hold a call;
  • to conclude and perform a service agreement, if you decide to work with the Controller;
  • to analyze traffic and improve the Website.

4. Legal grounds

Data is processed on the basis of your consent and for the conclusion and performance of an agreement to which you are or will be a party. You give consent by your actions: booking a call, contacting the Controller via a messenger or email, or continuing to use the Website after the cookie notice.

5. Disclosure to third parties

The Controller does not sell personal data and does not disclose it to third parties, except as described below.

Data is processed by the services through which you contact the Controller or which are used on the Website: Calendly (booking a call), Telegram, WhatsApp, MAX, email, and Yandex Metrica (analytics). These services operate under their own privacy policies. Data may also be disclosed upon a lawful request of government authorities.

The Controller does not carry out cross-border transfers of personal data.

6. Storage and protection

Data is stored no longer than necessary for the purposes of processing, or until you withdraw your consent. The Controller takes the necessary organizational and technical measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure and destruction.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • obtain information about how your data is processed;
  • request that your data be corrected, blocked or deleted;
  • withdraw your consent to processing;
  • file a complaint with the competent data protection authority (Roskomnadzor in Russia) or with a court.

To exercise your rights, write to svekochetova@gmail.com. Your request will be handled within the time limits set by law.

8. Changes to the Policy

The Controller may update this Policy. The current version is always available on this page, and the date of the last update is shown at the top.
