Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help remember your choices and collect anonymous visit statistics.

2. Which cookies and services are used

Necessary (technical). They remember that you have accepted the cookie notice so that it is not shown again. They are stored in your browser for up to 1 year.

Analytics.

  • Yandex Metrica (Yandex LLC) collects anonymous data about visits: referral sources, pages viewed, time on site, device and browser type. It sets its own cookies for this, for example _ym_uid and _ym_d. Learn more: Yandex Privacy Policy.
  • Google Search Console is a Google service that the website owner uses to check how the Website appears in Google Search. It works on Google’s side and does not set cookies on your device when you visit the Website.

The Website does not use advertising, marketing or personalization cookies.

3. Consent

A notice is shown when you first visit the Website. By clicking “Accept”, you agree to the use of the cookies listed above. Your choice is saved and the notice is no longer shown.

4. How to turn cookies off

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and others). The Website will keep working, but the cookie notice will appear on every visit.

You can opt out of Yandex Metrica statistics by following these instructions.

5. Third-party cookies

When you follow links to Calendly, Telegram, WhatsApp, MAX and other third-party sites, they may use their own cookies. The Website owner does not control them, so please read the policies of those services.

6. Contacts and more information

How personal data is processed is described in the Privacy Policy.
